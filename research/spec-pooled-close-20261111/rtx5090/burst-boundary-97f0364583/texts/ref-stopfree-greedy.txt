Planning and executing an orchard survey is a critical component of modern agricultural management, serving as the diagnostic foundation for crop health, yield prediction, and long-term sustainability. The process begins with meticulous pre-survey planning, where the primary objective is defined: whether to assess pest pressure, evaluate fruit quality, monitor tree vigor, or map canopy density.
