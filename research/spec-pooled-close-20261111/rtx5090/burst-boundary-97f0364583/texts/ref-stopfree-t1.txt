Conducting a comprehensive orchard survey is a critical component of integrated pest management and sustainable production. It is not a single event but a structured process designed to detect pests, diseases, and environmental stressors before they cause significant economic loss. The planning phase begins with defining the objectives, such as identifying active pest populations, assessing crop health, or verifying the efficacy of previous interventions.
