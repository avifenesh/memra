# Selected Eager/DecodeGraph caller qualification

This is a new, explicit scope approved for preparation after the frozen 3d1
three-surface campaign failed. It does not turn that failed 26-case plan into a
pass: only inspection ran successfully there, capture failed, and 24 later cases
were unrun. The original 4/8/16 prime inputs and fixed 0.005 policy are retained in
the historical record; the first four-token prime failed before the others ran.
Issue 585 owns the unqualified PrimeGraph numerical program.

The selected bundle may contain exactly `decode-eager` and `decode-graph`.
`carried-prime` remains absent. Neither a failed graph candidate nor success on a
different prime implementation can grant the shared CarriedPrime permission.
All captures and replays use the actual fresh owned executables, pinned source,
`MEMRA_FAST=0`, and `CUDA_FORCE_PRELOAD_LIBRARIES=1` supplied before child exec.
Library-set/hash checks, numerical settings and parity policy remain unchanged.

The final fallback measurement must not be inferred from the worker's four-token
control or from a route predicate. A numerical failure remains a failed case and
blocks completion of this selected phase. Any later fallback repair or explicit
refusal of an unsupported qualified program requires its own source review and
fresh evidence. The original plan changed no fallback implementation. The subsequent
`65510819` attempt completed all cases but failed the T16 fallback numerically
(26 passed, one failed), and its index remains quarantined. The isolated follow-up
routes Eager-only prime calls through the qualified T1 entry and preserves all
hidden rows; its source and native qualification are pending. PrimeGraph remains
excluded. The expanded measurements above live inside the same final required case.

The selected bundle is provisional throughout the run. Any case, finalization or
publication exception quarantines its installable index and reseals the failed
evidence tree. This also covers an exception after atomic PASS replacement. A
pre-existing output directory is never modified by failure cleanup. If evidence
resealing itself fails, the result remains failed with no new manifest claim.

The original positive retained-prime library/environment cells stay unqualified;
the new refusal cells are not substitutes for claiming they passed. The original
three-surface mode remains separate. Existing baseline admission, generic battery,
transfer, router, Gemma and lifecycle/cancellation gates retain their scope.

Implementation, CPU controls and independent review precede the new native run.
There is no current selected-phase/native/merge GO in this document.
