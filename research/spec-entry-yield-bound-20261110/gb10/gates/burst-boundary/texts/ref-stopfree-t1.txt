Conducting a comprehensive orchard survey is a critical component of integrated pest management and sustainable production. It is not a passive walk; it is a structured, data-driven exercise designed to translate field observations into actionable decisions. The process generally follows three distinct phases: planning, execution, and analysis.

**Planning Phase**
The foundation of a successful survey lies in rigorous preparation. First, define the objectives: Are you scouting for specific diseases like apple scab, monitoring pest populations
