--- title: Lab Operator type: entity tags: [role, operations, cloud, research] sources: [cloud-cost-discipline, working-style-lead-helper, streaming-not-batch, research-rigor-contract, hardware-local-rig] updated: 2026-07-11 --- # Lab Operator The operator role for a single-person inference lab: one person who owns the hardware budget, the serving stack, and the research agenda at the same time. A fleet of running accelerator instances is a normal working state for this role rather than a sign of forgotten spend, because capacity is provisioned per experiment and released when the experiment ends (see [[cloud-cost-discipline]]). The role keeps separate cloud identities for administration and for model access, on the principle that a credential able to launch hardware should not be the same credential an application uses to call a model API. ## How the role works - The operator leads and the assistant helps — never the other way round. The operator is a paying customer of the tools, not a free sandbox, and wants to do and feel the work rather than receive a finished artifact (see [[working-style-lead-helper]]). - The systems mental model is STREAMING, not BATCH: stages run concurrently and outputs flow as they are produced, so nothing phase-waits on a predecessor that has already emitted what the next stage needs. - Research ethos: prove limits empirically and never assume them from prior literature; days-to-weeks is an acceptable cost, because correctness is the constraint and speed is not (see [[research-rigor-contract]]). ## Models
