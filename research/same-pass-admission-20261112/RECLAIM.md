# Partial reclaim with an unbuilt session (memra#1255 review round 2)

`harness/reclaim-cell.py` through `harness/reclaim-5090.sh` inside a `gpulock` lease, on the RTX
5090 Laptop GPU (power limit recorded in each `cell.log`, not changed). The cell that reaches the
door-off owed terms on the device; no other run in this directory does.

Shape: Qwen3.8-27B NVFP4 + Q5_K, DFlash2 drafter, `MEMRA_CTX` 8,192, 4 interactive sessions,
judge and harvest lanes capped at 1 (`MEMRA_LANE_MAX_JUDGE=1`, `MEMRA_LANE_MAX_HARVEST=1`), no
`MEMRA_PREFIX_CACHE_MB`: the derived prefix budget is 2,815 MB, above its two-entry floor of 800 MB,
so `reclaim_partial_applies` and a short reclaim evicts only what the need asks. Learn passes for
A (2,004 tokens) and B (7,343 tokens) and two fill prompts leave four prefix entries; a decoding
keeper (counting run); a ballast places effective free at 2,280 MB, between A's need (362 MB +
reserve) and B's (893 MB + reserve). The pair uses fresh text (no prefix hit, so A is a cold
DFlash prime with no route state): A, then B 20 ms later. One run per row; generated by
`harness/reclaim-table.py`.

argument instead of deriving it): the same decisions, the same numbers. `fix5-r6` is the PR head.

The reclaim lines (`serve.log.gz` in each run):

- base: `reclaim-on-defer (partial): evicted 3 prefix entries (861MB, oldest first) for 681MB
  (416MB of it owed by 1 priming sessions and the arrival's own entry); kept 2 unleased entries
  (440MB); effective free 2280MB -> 3142MB; covered`
- head: `reclaim-on-defer (partial): evicted 4 prefix entries (1131MB, oldest first) for 1001MB
  (375MB of it owed by 1 priming sessions and the arrival's own entry); kept 1 unleased entries
  (170MB); effective free 1919MB -> 3050MB; covered`

Base books nothing for A and counts it in the owed term by its call and returned rows (41 MB of
the 416; 375 MB is B's own entry). The head books A's whole charge once through the unbuilt
booking (its effective free reads 361 MB lower) and leaves A out of the owed term. Both admitted
B in A's pass on the DFlash route; no out-of-memory line in any run.
