Primary-paper check: the residual-stream oracle is real (`Q_pred^(i+1) = W_Q^(i+1) X^i`,
0.93-0.97 query cosine similarity), but the headline path is approximate CPU/GPU sparse
co-attention, not an exact prefetch-only scheduler. Reported cells are 5.1x FullKV at 64k,
up to 2.1x over prior offload, 6% GPU idle, and 2.1-2.5% LongBench accuracy loss. The survey
therefore records ScoutAttention as a default-OFF lossy research door and **noted, not a
near-term lane**: memra currently has flat GPU-resident KV rather than a KV pager, its analogous
expert-prefetch oracle was already negative on lead-time versus I/O, and current Step/122B
capacity pressure is concurrent-session-shaped rather than a single 64k KV-over-VRAM cliff.

Forecast, 33.5M new parameters per 8B model (0.41%), KL-only Forecast training, and a persistent

both published mechanisms research-only for distinct reasons and carries forward only their
lookahead scheduling contract into the frozen-byte, exact-attention posture.
