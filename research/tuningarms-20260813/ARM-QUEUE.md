# Tuning arm queue — pod A (Qwen3.6-27B NVFP4) and pod B (Step-3.7-Flash), 2026-08-13

Two 3-card RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell Server Edition boxes: **sm_120a, 188 SM, 600 W, 97,887 MiB per card.**
Pod A runs dense Qwen3.6-27B NVFP4 + MTP drafter (proxy for the imminent 3.8-27B). Pod B runs
Step-3.7-Flash, 196B/10.3B-active sigmoid-router MoE, resident across the 3 cards.

Every arm below cites a code seam, states its exactness gate, and states what falsifies it. No arm's
expected value rests on transferring a measurement from the 82-SM 5090 or the 132-SM H100.

## Binding constraints (an arm that breaks one of these is not in this queue)

1. **One numeric program per request.** No path may emit tokens for a single request under two
   numerical programs unless the crossing is forbidden or proven bit-identical. Two production
   defects had exactly this cause (`research/eosclass-20260813/`, `research/splitiso-20260813/`).
   Every arm here either makes its decision once before token 0 or is schedule-only.
2. **Winners are defaults.** Env vars are rollback/measurement seams only. Every arm's end state is
   a default — SM-keyed, model-keyed, or unconditional — not a flag an operator must find.
3. **Exactness gates precede perf claims.** kernel-check + `run-gen` argmax + `run-spec` K=1..8 are
   the floor; serving-shape arms additionally need a served exact-token cell, because all three unit
   gates were green while `MEMRA_MOE_GROUPED` truncated served completions at 25/60.
4. **One scored campaign per box, behind `flock /tmp/memra-gpu.lock`.** The cards share a `PIX`
   PCIe path; a second tenant on an "idle" card changes the thermal and I/O regime.
   `research/cachesize-20260813/raw/attempt1-gpu1-overlap/` is a discarded full attempt of the top
   money lane, lost exactly this way. The other cards may take build, compile, staging, and short
   non-timed pass/fail cells only.
5. **Never commit nsys/ncu profiles** (gitignored + pre-push guarded).
6. **`research/tune-data/pro6000wk-runpod-community.jsonl` is a retired 510 W community board** that
   measured 5-11% under prod-class silicon with memory-clock droop. Its numbers are valid as relative
   deltas and as motivation; they may not be re-minted or quoted as prod numbers. RunPod is closed.

Ranking key: `(expected gain) / (effort x exactness risk)`. Risk is 1 (schedule-only or
bit-identical by construction), 2 (needs a served exact-token cell), 3 (touches restore/KV bytes or
a program boundary the repo has already been burned on).

(`worker.rs:6742`) is unreachable at PP-3: `admission_headroom` early-returns `Primary` whenever
`dual_stages` is None, and that is gated on the **dual-active door**, not on `stages == 2` — so
per-device accounting is equally absent at PP-2-serial, and generalizing to `>= 2` is a literal
no-op (the cost model is hardcoded 2-stage by type: `[DualPpStageAdmission; 2]`,
`dual_pp_device_requirements(devices: [usize; 2], ..)`). The direction is also backwards: the Primary
branch charges the **full aggregate** session KV against the head card, which the stage numbers
partition, so it defers too early — lost capacity, not silent remote step-OOM. The spec-gate half of
that arm is real and survives as **B1**.

`MEMRA_PP_ALLOW_UNSPLIT_BATCH` (measurement door only: 28x cliff at B=1 on 2x PRO 6000, exactness

## Open questions needing a measurement before an arm can be designed

1. **Is pod B actually running 3 stages?** Every shipped Step example is PP-2 (`README.md:147`,
   `tools/step35-prime-batch-gate.sh:70`, `docs/SERVING.md:288`) and every serve receipt is
   `stages=2, devices=0,1`. Grep the boot log for `[spec-gate] policy placement=` and the resident
   numerator line. If it is PP-2 with a third card idle, B1 has no defect and B2/B4/B6 change scope.
2. **A PP-3 serial baseline for Step-3.7 — there is none in the repo**, and no PP-3 bit-identity cell
   either. B2, B4 and B6 all need it as their denominator. Cheapest first cell on pod B.
3. **`cudaDevAttrMaxSharedMemoryPerBlockOptin` on the Server Edition die.** ~99 KiB (the GB202 class
   value) makes B7 a two-width sweep; anything >114 KiB makes X=128 a real cell and is itself the
   finding.
4. **Which cache shape does real traffic have?** SLRU's 0%-vs-75% collapse needs a complete disjoint
   cohort turnover; pod bench traffic is whatever the harness scripts. C5's falsifier can only be
   evaluated on the two-card pair, and `/metrics` publishes no segment/promotion/demotion
   counters today.
5. **Q27 c=1 spec ON/OFF on-box, in one interleaved N=5 series.** Nothing in the repo bounds the spec
   win that A4 gives up on a hit, and the q9 374.8/224.5 pair may not be used for it.
6. **Step-3.7 KV bytes at the sold context on two cards.** The weights fit is receipted (45.72 + 3.92
   vs 100.88 GB free per stage); KV at target ctx is the only open term in the PP-2-vs-PP-3 decision.
7. **Pod B's prefill rate under a legal configuration.** The concurrent-prefill QoS numbers (7.0 s
   serial prefill, 0.7 tok/s aggregate, TTFT p95 28.4 s at c=4) were measured with
   `MEMRA_MOE_GROUPED=1`, now blocked and known unsafe. C2's pod-B magnitude is unmeasured until this
   is re-run.
8. **Task #124 (one-card cache-capacity campaign on the repaired tip) is upstream of A4, C2 and C3.**
   Its predecessor failed closed with 11/60-token restored hits, so until it is green every hit cell
   in those three arms is contaminated.
9. **Does `MEMRA_FA_SPW` still want 24 at 188 SM?** `docs/FLAGS.md:151` records a +4% PRO measurement
   at `24` with **no committed raw log anywhere in `research/`** — an evidence-discipline gap as well
   as an unclaimed per-hardware default. Scope is gemma-windowed, so it bears on the third listed
   model rather than either pod; SPW must stay uniform across decode and verify (a t-keyed probe broke
   combine-order parity).
10. **NVFP4 vs FP8-ST on one interleaved decode board on the PRO.** Independent SM120 reports have
    NVFP4 losing to FP8 on essentially every decode cell despite half the weight bandwidth; memra
    ships both residencies for the same 27B (`MEMRA_ST_E4M3` +2.58%, 430 MiB freed) and has never put
    them on one board on this die. Pod A is being tuned as an NVFP4 artifact on an untested premise.
11. **`docs/FLAGS.md` coverage is worse than it admits** — 545 distinct `MEMRA_*` literals in
    `crates/` vs 448 mentioned in the file (its own self-count of 456/434 is stale). At least
    `MEMRA_KV_PREFETCH` (`decode.rs:2662-2666`, declared value-free, zero verdict on any rig),
    `MEMRA_MMQ_X_IQEXP`, `MEMRA_MMQ_X_Q4`, `MEMRA_MOE_PREFETCH_TOP`/`_MIN_LAYER`, `MEMRA_FA_BF16KV`,
    `MEMRA_LOCKSTEP_M`, `MEMRA_Q80_MR`, `MEMRA_RMS_BLOCK` are undocumented non-diagnostic tuning
    seams. Grep before concluding a seam is absent.
