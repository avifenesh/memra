# dspark-q38-train — clean-license DSpark drafter for Qwen3.8-27B (opened 2026-08-15)

## Goal

Serve Qwen3.8-27B on OpenRouter with speculative decoding. The existing
`RadixArk/Qwen3.8-27B-DSpark` checkpoint (published 2026-08-14, SGLang org, SpecForge-trained)
carries `license: other` with no license file — **unusable in production until clarified**. So:

1. **Control eval** (this lane, runs now): RadixArk drafter via SGLang on a rented PRO 6000 —
   research control only, never the deliverable. Measures the acceptance ceiling on OUR serving
   shapes (agentic/tool-call, chat, long-context; think + nothink), which their published evals
   do not cover (weakest published numbers are exactly chat-class: Arena-Hard 2.71, MT-Bench 3.10).
2. **Own training** (this lane): SpecForge (or DeepSpec) DSpark drafter trained on an owned corpus
   with responses regenerated by `Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B-FP8` (apache-2.0). Output = our checkpoint,
   our license. Rig (owner call 2026-08-15): **ONE machine for training + eval + bench —
   cloud-8card (8x RTX PRO 6000, $33.14/hr US) preferred, cloud-4card (4x, $16.57/hr) fallback.**
   Rationale: eval/bench must be on PRO 6000 anyway (target hardware), the target model is frozen
   during drafter training (inference-only forward, small drafter gradients — PCIe acceptable),
   and one box kills the per-step machine-setup tax. p5/H100 stays the fallback only if PRO 6000
   training throughput proves too slow. Scored bench cells SERIALIZE behind `/tmp/memra-gpu.lock`
   on this box per evidence discipline — no timed measurement while training occupies sibling
   cards; untimed correctness checks only.
3. **Engine port** (owner's lane, NOT this one): dflash.rs Qwen3.8 wiring. This lane feeds it the
   checkpoint + parity fixtures and stays out of its way.

## Division of labor

- Owner: dflash.rs development (engine port), all merge/tag calls, OR submission.
- This lane: control-eval harness + receipts, corpus build, training runs, acceptance evals,
  drafter GGUF/artifact handoff.

## Hard rules honored

- RadixArk checkpoint: diagnostics/control ONLY (no-format-compatibility-shortcuts analog for
  licensing: fallback artifacts must not become the deliverable).
- SGLang/vLLM/torch: research controls + training harness only. Nothing external ships in memra.
- Rigs: control eval + training on NEW rented boxes (PRO 6000 catch in flight; H100 tier for training).
  NEVER the tuning pods (Pod A owns Q38 tuning), never Vast, never the b5 pair.
- Acceptance is measured against the artifact memra actually serves (NVFP4+Q5_K mint), not
  assumed from FP8-target numbers — per no-generic-support-claims.
- Evidence: raw JSONL/logs land under this directory next to every summary row.

## Corpus (training)

Base: `mlabonne/open-perfectblend` mix via the dspark2 pilot's frozen builder
(`research/dspark2-20260811/build-prompt-pack.py`, seed 20260811, quotas chat/math/code/if)
EXTENDED with:
- agentic/tool-call shapes (the serving class; builder already has `--agentic-dir`),
- long-context extension shapes (Q38 native 262k is the OR pitch; GDN-hybrid prefix-cache
  reuse is extension-only, so long-ctx acceptance matters),
- think AND nothink modes (DeepSpec warns thinking-mode targets need matched corpus).
Responses regenerated by Qwen3.8-27B-FP8 — no teacher-response copying, no third-party ToS
exposure. Exact sizes/format after SpecForge recon lands.

## Distinctions from the killed dspark2 pilot (2026-08-11)

That kill (q2=0.1043 vs >=0.60 bar) was a from-scratch 318M memra-built drafter + own loss on a
2K-pair smoke against Qwen3.5-9B. This lane uses the published, working SpecForge recipe and
architecture (1.36B, 5 layers, taps, Markov+confidence) that demonstrably produced a functioning
Q38 drafter two days ago. Not a resume of the frozen kill; a different program. The kill rule on
the 30K memra-harness continuation stands untouched.

## Gates

- G1 control eval: RadixArk acceptance per workload class on PRO 6000, spec ON/OFF tok/s, raw JSONL.
- G2 training smoke: loss curves + heldout acceptance on a 2K slice before full corpus (kill early).
- G3 own drafter >= RadixArk acceptance on agentic + chat classes (the corpus-match bet), and
  absolute accept_len >= ~3 on serving classes (else spec may not pay — decide with owner).
- G4 acceptance re-measured against the memra-served NVFP4 mint (owner's dflash.rs lane consumes).
