The proposed architecture utilizes a **Rust-based host runtime** for high-performance scheduling, KV-cache management, and expert offloading, leveraging `cudarc` for direct, low-overhead access to the CUDA driver and libraries. Kernel development will rely on **hand-written `.cu` kernels** compiled via `nvcc` into a fatbin, specifically targeting the `sm_120a` architecture to unlock Blackwell-specific block-scaled FP4/FP8 acceleration. The entire system is packaged as a **native, in-process `axum`/`tokio` server**, eliminating the Python dispatch and memory
