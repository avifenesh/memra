## Summary

On the local RTX 5090 Laptop GPU a growing `prompt_ids` conversation served through the prefix cache produces different bytes from the cold prime of the same prompt, deterministically, at greedy near-ties. The restored render and the cold render are two numeric programs: the cold prime's `prime_cache` calls all start on the 32-token GDN WY-chunk grid, the restored render restores at the prompt-end seed boundary (an arbitrary position) and primes the suffix from there off the grid. The engine's own law (`align_prime_ranges_to_gdn`, `crates/memra-engine/src/hybrid_forward.rs`) says that split is bit-identical to the monolithic prime iff the call start is a multiple of `gdn_chunk_size()`; the worker aligns the LCP-split and message-boundary captures to that grid (`grid_align_boundary_within`) but the prompt-end seed (`maybe_prefix_seed`, `insert (seed)`) is captured wherever the prompt ends. Owner law (memra CLAUDE.md, "One numeric program per request"): a restored suffix must produce the same bytes as the cold prime, on every card class.

## Why other gates are green

`tools/spec-on-cache-hit-gate.sh qwen` compares full-cover hits (empty suffix, no suffix prime call) with their cold leader, spec-on with spec-off restores, or two suffix hits with each other; it never compares a suffix-fed restored completion with the cold render of the same prompt. The twin gate records identity per turn and does not judge it. The target card's day-14 8/8 and day-15 28/28 were near-ties that did not flip there, not program identity: the class is card-independent, which request flips is card-dependent.

## Fix direction (a lane of its own; nothing changed today)

The receipts point at the prompt-end seed's capture position: align it down to the grid the way the LCP and message-boundary captures already are (`grid_align_boundary_within`, which also keeps the tail at or above `PRIME_MIN_T`), so a hit's suffix prime call starts on the grid and reproduces the monolithic fold; then make the twin gate's `== cold` column a verdict so the class cannot come back silently. Any alternative must satisfy the same law: the restored render's prime call starts must be multiples of `gdn_chunk_size()` under the chunked scan.
