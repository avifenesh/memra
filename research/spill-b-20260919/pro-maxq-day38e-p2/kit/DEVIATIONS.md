# Deviations from the registered cell (DAY38 addendum D on the Workstation, box-d)

The registered cell: `research/spill-b-20260919/pro-single-day38/chain-d.sh` at the harness tree `3476ea4dca`,
arms built by `build-arms.sh` from `a803d3080`, boots by `day38d-run.sh` through `run-day26-cell.sh`, read by
`day38d-read.py pro6000`. Unchanged here: the client (`day38-client.py`), the per-boot parser (`day38-parse.py`)
and the reader (`day38d-read.py`), byte for byte; the prompt text; the boot names and arm env of the seven rule
boots; lengths, shapes, N, `max_tokens`, `max_ctx` padding, greedy decoding, port 18526, the server env of 1.1.
`PORTABILITY.diff` is the full diff of the two ported scripts.
