     CORRECTION to this row's first draft: the 42 per-layer router DtoH + stream syncs are NOT
     recoverable by the epilogue and never were. The sigmoid router is ALREADY a device kernel;
     what crosses the bus is the tiny [t, n_used] sel/w, and the host needs `sel` because it
     DRIVES ADMISSION. The zero-DtoH path needs a device pointer table, which exists only for a
     fully resident layer (layer_dev_row / prewarm_layer) or a load-time slab. One glm5 MoE
     layer is 288x3 = 864 blocks against ~285 slots per layer at the serving recipe's 12000, so
     no layer can be fully resident on this placement. The sync removal is therefore DOWNSTREAM
     OF STEP 3, not part of step 4. Source: lane/glm53-epilogue finding 1, 2026-08-28.

     The KDA (~600), mHC pre/post (~540) and MLA chains are NOT touched by the epilogue, so the
     residue still needs its own launch-structure arc — and CUDA graph capture, the usual
     answer, is refuse_hyper'd on this residual topology.

STEP 3 — what "full expert residency" actually requires (source-level sizing)
  171.2 GB of routed experts vs 2 x 96 GB. Card 0 alone holds at most ~57-66 GB of them
  (12000-14000 SLRU slots = 33-39% of the 36,288 blocks), so single-card residency is
  arithmetically impossible; the second card is the only route, and it is idle at 3 MiB today.
  With MEMRA_BF16_MMV the trunk halves to 13.9 GB, and an even 21/21 layer split puts ~85.6 GB
  of experts + ~7 GB of trunk on each card — inside 96 GB, but only with the BF16 trunk.
  So step 2 is not merely 6 ms; it is what makes step 3 fit.
