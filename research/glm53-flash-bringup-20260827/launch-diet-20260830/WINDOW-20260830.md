launches/token). On the SERVING arm (arm C + fused epi) the launch/gap term

~139 ms/layer-chunk. The attention math at that width is a few-ms-class

MLA prefill chain (gathered/absorbed form running at matvec-class
efficiency over prefill widths), not scheduling, not the indexer
(17.5 ms), not MoE, not KDA. It is also consistent with upstream: both
vLLM/SGLang run MLA prefill through FlashMLA-class tensor-core kernels.
