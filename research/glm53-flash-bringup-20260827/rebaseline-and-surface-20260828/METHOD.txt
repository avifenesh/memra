  rawprobe.py            NEW. /v1/completions, so NO chat template is involved. This is the

  vprobe.py              NEW. VENDOR-DEFAULT SAMPLED: the body carries NO temperature, NO top_p,

                         reasoning_effort pinned. Banks the full reasoning and content text so

  serve.sh               NEW. PID-VERIFIED restart. The banked cell2.sh uses `pkill -x

                         so serve.sh resolves /proc/<pid>/exe, refuses anything that is not a
                         memra-server, excludes itself, waits, and escalates only after
                         re-verifying the same pid AND the same exe.
  battery.py             NEW. The post-deploy standard-surface battery. Every tool cycle runs a
                         REAL local function whose return value (21 C, sunny, 11 kph) appears in
                         no prompt, so a final answer containing it can only have come from the

  followup.py            NEW. The decisive probes the battery's failures demanded.
  renderdiff.py          NEW. Offline render attribution against the checkpoint's own
                         chat_template.jinja under the transformers jinja env.
  ctxprobe.py            NEW. Context ladder, 1k..7.9k tokens, greedy, one word of output.
  coldrestore.py         NEW. Cold prefill vs prefix-cache restore, arms interleaved rep by rep.
  degenrate.py           NEW. The same separation applied to degeneration rate.

PROTOCOL.
  Interleaving. The only cross-binary PERFORMANCE claim in this lane is "the p5 greedy rate did
  not move", and it is carried by a boot-level A,B,A,B interleave: OLD1 13:23, NEW1 13:43,
  OLD2 13:46, NEW2 13:51. DEVIATION FROM x5, stated rather than hidden, and the same deviation
  the parent lane's METHOD.txt records: these arms are whole binaries, so the interleave unit is
  a BOOT, not a request, and four boots at ~2-4 minutes of model load each was the budget the
  box window allowed. Within-boot spread is under 4% and the cross-arm difference is 0.09%, so
  the claim made is "unchanged within spread" and no delta is asserted.

  Cold versus restored. BOX-STATE.txt already records that on this model a COLD first request
  differs in bytes from a prefix-restored one. Every comparison here is therefore
  restored-to-restored or cold-to-cold, and where the distinction is the SUBJECT rather than a
  nuisance (coldrestore.py, degenrate.py) the two arms are interleaved rep by rep and a unique
  per-request nonce is what forces the cold arm.

  Greedy is the instrument, never the product. Greedy carries the identity oracles (raw
  discriminator shas, the effort ladder, the re-baseline tape). Every quality and serving
  statement is carried by the vendor-default SAMPLED arm.

      No perf row in this lane comes from that window.
  (b) From ~13:55 UTC, a throttled, ionice'd 178 GB artifact transfer OFF this box to stand up a
      second rented box, coordinated by the lane coordinator. It was HELD at this lane's request until
      the interleaved timing pair finished at 13:55, so ALL FOUR timing boots (OLD1, NEW1, OLD2,
      NEW2) are outside it. Everything after 13:55 in this lane is correctness work, surface
      battery, follow-ups, context ladder, cold/restored, degeneration rate, where a disk and
      network load cannot change a status code, a token count, a tool call or an output sha.
      The one measurement that could in principle be perturbed is model LOAD time, and it shows
      it: boots before the window took 128-134 s, the RINGOFF boot inside it took 134 s and the
      later ones 94 s. Load time is not a claim in this lane.
