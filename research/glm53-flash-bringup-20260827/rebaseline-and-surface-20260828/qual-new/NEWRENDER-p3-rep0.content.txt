Solid recovery. A few notes on your follow-ups and details:

**On the diagnosis being right:** The lazy-open failure mode from a deleted venv is sneaky because the process looks healthy — it was started before the venv vanished, so it serves workers fine until it tries to lazy-load anything it hasn't already opened (TLS bundles, late imports). That's why SIGTERM-on-PID + fresh venv + relaunch was the correct fix rather than trying to "heal" the original process.

**On the systemd unit — worth doing.** A sketch:

```ini
# ~/.config/systemd/user/hermes-gateway.service
[Unit]
Description=Hermes gateway
After=network-online.target

[Service]
WorkingDirectory=/home/avifenesh/path/to/repo
ExecStart=%h/path/to/repo/.venv/bin/python -m gateway.run
Restart=on-failure
RestartSec=5

[Install]
WantedBy=default.target
```

Then `systemctl --user enable --now hermes-gateway`, and add `loginctl enable-linger avifenesh` if you want it alive before you log in. You lose the tmux attach workflow, but you can get equivalent coverage with `journalctl --user -u hermes-gateway -f` — or keep tmux purely as an interactive/debugging surface and let systemd own the process.

**On the error surface fix** — agreed, and I'd go slightly further:

```python
msg = f"{e}"
if isinstance(e, FileNotFoundError) and e.filename:
    msg = f"{e.filename}: {e}"
```

`str(FileNotFoundError)` normally *includes* the path, so if it's being stripped, that suggests the catch-all wraps/librarifies the exception somewhere or a subclass has no `filename` populated. Checking whether the `ENOTDIR`-style path info survives the wrapping chain might save you the next debug session entirely.

**On the lockfile issue:** worth a ticket rather than living with `--frozen`. The pattern of "re-resolve breaks because of the cutoff date + Python 3.14 in the supported set" will keep biting anyone who runs `uv sync` without the flag. Options: bump `exclude-newer`, drop 3.14 from `requires-python`/supported set until your cutoff allows it, or add a preflight check that errors loudly with "use --frozen" instead of failing mid-resolve.
