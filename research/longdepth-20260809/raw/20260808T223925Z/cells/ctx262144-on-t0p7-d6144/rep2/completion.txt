<section id="s0004">
<h2>4. Safety Overview</h2>
<p>Personnel must follow lockout-tagout procedures before servicing physical hardware. Electrical hazards exist in power distribution units and battery backup systems.</p>
<p>Report all incidents within 24 hours using the incident management portal. Near-miss events also require documentation to improve safeguards.</p>
<dl>
<dt>PPE</dt><dd>Personal protective equipment</dd>
<dt>LOTO</dt><dd>Lockout-tagout</dd>
