<p>Failover to the secondary region is automated for critical services. Manual intervention is required for data consistency checks.</p>
<dl>
<dt>RTO</dt><dd>4 hours</dd>
<dt>RPO</dt><dd>15 minutes</dd>
<dt>Testing</dt><dd>Semi-annual</dd>
<dt>Automation</dt><dd>Partial</dd>
