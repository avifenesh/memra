<h2><span class="num">Section 2.</span> Safety and Hazard Classification</h2>
<p>Hazard levels range from L0 (informational) to L4 (catastrophic). Operators must verify PPE before engaging L2 or higher tasks. Log entries are mandatory for any deviation.</p>
<p>The safety officer may elevate or reduce hazard levels based on situational assessment. All changes require a signed paper or digital record within 24 hours.</p>
<dl>
<dt>L0</dt><dd>Informational, no hazard.</dd>
<dt>L1</dt><dd>Caution, minor risk.</dd>
<dt>L2</dt><dd>Warning, possible injury.</dd>
<dt>L3</dt><dd>Critical, severe injury likely.</dd>

<h2><span class="num">Section 5.</span> System Overview</h2>
<p>Atlas consists of control nodes, worker nodes, storage arrays, and network fabric. Each layer has independent monitoring and failover. The control plane manages orchestration.</p>
<p>Uptime target is 99.99% annually. Planned maintenance windows are published quarterly. Emergency maintenance is logged and reviewed.</p>

<dt>Worker</dt><dd>Compute layer.</dd>
<dt>Storage</dt><dd>Data layer.</dd>
<dt>Network</dt><dd>Connectivity layer.</dd>
