Terms of service

draft · 2026-08-09

1. Operator

Avi Fenesh currently operates the tiyuvta website and API trial. The registered-business details will be added when registration is complete.

2. Current trial service

The trial serves stepfun/step-3.7-flash through an OpenAI-style API. The live catalog reports a 262,144-token context window and text input to text output. Trial access is limited, may be interrupted, and has no service-level agreement or uptime guarantee.

3. Access, limits, and abuse

Keys are issued manually and carry a limit shared at issuance. Do not publish or resell a trial key, evade its limit, interfere with the service, probe another user's data, or use the API for unlawful activity.

Access may be limited or revoked when a key is abused or the trial needs to stop. There is no automated signup or entitlement to a key.

4. Your data

Data handling is governed by the privacy & data policy. In short: operational metadata is retained, and trial prompts and responses may be retained and used for training or evaluation. Do not send data that is unsuitable for that policy.

5. Billing

The current trial costs $0. There is no card charge, invoice, prepaid balance, or automatic conversion to a paid plan. Paid prices and binding payment terms are not published. See trial pricing.

6. Do not rely on the trial for critical work

The trial is provided for evaluation. It has no uptime, latency, determinism, output-quality, or continued-availability guarantee. Keep a fallback and do not use it as the only path for safety-critical or production-critical work.

7. Changes

The trial catalog, limits, and availability may change. The live /v1/models response is the current catalog. Binding terms will carry an effective date when they are ready.
