## S13: engine FFI shims, vision, MiMo, KDA and remaining engine files

**What the slice is.** Fifty-six memra-engine files in four groups:

1. **Shared numeric FFI shims.** `mmq_ffi.rs`, `fp8_ffi.rs`, `f16_ffi.rs` and `cutlass_ffi.rs` hold the vendored and tensor-core prefill GEMMs that `Engine::matmul` reaches for every m>=GEMM_M_THRESHOLD projection.
2. **glm5_next-only mixers.** `kda.rs` (Kimi Delta Attention) and `mla_ffi.rs` (MLA latent attention plus the DSA k-pool indexer).
3. **Vision.** One tower per family, the shared overlay splice, and host preprocessing.
4. **Infrastructure and diagnostics.** Prime walker and odometer, the grid capture, the step guard, spec round streams, env audit, the sigmoid-router contracts, and 26 MiMo diagnostic modules.

**Hot paths per family and route.**
- **Dense prefill GEMM classes** (all families). `mmq_supports` picks the MMQ tile by format (mmq_ffi.rs:1484-1541): NVFP4 W4A8, Q4_K/Q5_K, Q8_0, Q4_0 and IQ4_XS are default ON, and W4A4 NVFP4 needs `MEMRA_MMQ=1` (1583). `matmul` tries FP8 (`try_fp8_gemm`, `try_fp8_blk_mmq`) and the f16 mirror (`try_f16_gemm`) first. Batched decode with 16 or more rows and dflash verify blocks also cross the threshold, so these arms are not prefill-only. The labels keep routes at `*`.
- **Format arms.** Q4_0 stream-K (1917-1960) is a numeric fold-order choice. It is default ON except on Hopper, and it is forced to tiling at load when `MEMRA_DRAFT` is set (a dynamic edge through `MMQ_SK_FORCE`). The calibrated A4 prefill (2196-2329) reaches only qwen35 27B NVFP4 artifacts.
- **MoE.** `moe_f16_grouped` (2712) is the grouped prefill lane for every MoE family. Mode 2 with the direct loaders is the default (2729). Mode 1 cuBLAS is opt-in (2809).
- **glm5_next.** `kda.rs` and `mla_ffi.rs` reach only glm5_next and glm_dsa, through the hyper prime/decode, glm_spec verify and glm5 TP walks. The entries are `kda_prime_cached`, `kda_decode_cached`, `kda_verify_rows_cached` and `mla_attn_gathered`.
- **Vision.** `EmbedOverlay::splice_into` (vision.rs:344) is the one splice every vision family runs. The towers are family-local:

| File | Family | Door |
|---|---|---|
| vision.rs | qwen35, qwen35_moe | `MEMRA_VISION_DIR` |
| vision_gemma.rs | gemma4 | `MEMRA_GEMMA_VISION` |
| vision_glm5.rs | glm5_next | default ON |
| vision_step.rs | step35 | `MEMRA_STEP_VISION_DIR` |

- **MiMo.** All 26 `mimo_*` files are reached only from mimo probe bins and from each other, so they have no serving route. The model.rs and hybrid.rs edges into `mimo_moe_load` are test `TensorSource` impls.

**Device-specific parts.**
- **B200 (sm_100a).** The B200-only arms sit behind `cfg!(memra_sm100_tcgen05)` or a built arch of `100a`:
  - DSA decode level 2 by default. It includes a numeric class change, warp-online gathered attention at t_q=1 (mla_ffi.rs:2909).
  - The bit-identical single-pass arm at t_q=4 (2942).
  - The multi-CTA k-pool select (2644).
  - KDA conv3 and o_norm zq8 (kda.rs:919, 343).
  - The block-FP8 MMQ refusal (mmq_ffi.rs:2357, fp8_ffi.rs:264).
- **Hopper.** wgmma Q8_0 (1597), the f16 mirror default (f16_ffi.rs:119), and stream-K OFF.
- **Ada (sm89).** `portable_mma_gated` turns off all MMQ and FP8 GEMMs.
- **120a.** CUTLASS is a build-time-only 120a door (cutlass_ffi.rs:11). The f16 mirror outside Hopper is keyed on free VRAM (f16_ffi.rs:135), so on 120a and B200 it depends on the box, not on a flag.

**Effect kinds.** The FFI shims and towers are numeric. The split, select and scorer doors are speed-only, because their own receipts show them bit-identical. prime_walker, progress, step_guard and env_audit are policy. spec_phase, moesd, moe_sel_dump, prime_receipt and cpu_probe are observability. `mla.rs` (CPU oracle), `o2_band.rs`, `parity_geometry.rs` and `prime_graph.rs` have no serving reach.

**Where the impact set is wide, and why.**
- `mmq_ffi.rs`, `f16_ffi.rs` and `fp8_ffi.rs` are shared by every family, and the format arms are what narrow them.
- `forward.rs` pairs a bin-only reference forward with the `argmax` that every greedy spec route calls.
- `vision.rs` pairs a qwen-only tower with the overlay machinery that all six vision families use.

Splitting those three, as the decoupling findings propose, would remove most false-positive cells.

**Top findings.**
1. `MEMRA_MLA_DECODE_SPLIT` (mla_ffi.rs:57). docs/FLAGS.md:953 records a receipt-backed flip to default ON, but the code still requires `=1`. The flip was likely lost in a sync, so glm5_next decode runs without it on every card.
2. Q4_0 stream-K fixup scratch (mmq_ffi.rs:25). It is one process-global buffer reused by every engine and device. Gemma PP2 and multi-model boots share partial sums across cards.
3. The quantize-once activation window (mmq_ffi.rs:1836) never closes, so "paths that never call this never hit the cache" is false.
4. `MEMRA_KPOOL_SELECT_V2` is past its 2026-09-20 decide-by date.
5. Several B200 door comments contradict their defaults (mla_ffi.rs:246, 353, 464; kda.rs:951). So do the Q8_0 MMQ comments (mmq_ffi.rs:1418, 1516).
6. The f16 mirror budget is process-global across devices and models (f16_ffi.rs:807).
7. Dead wrappers: `mla_dsa_attn_warp_online`, `kda_gate`, `quantize_fp4_act_cutlass`, the a4 accessors and the gemma/glm5 `prep_data_uri` functions.
