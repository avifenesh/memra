## S01: memra-engine lib.rs, lines 1-19197 (Engine core)

**What it is.** This is the root of the engine crate. It holds:
- about 150 `MEMRA_*` door resolvers and the per-model numeric statics;
- the `Engine` struct and `Engine::new`, which run the arch guard, pin the mem-pool threshold, load eight fatbin modules and turn off event tracking (lib.rs:3731-3870);
- the string-keyed kernel resolver `Engine::func` (lib.rs:4109);
- several hundred thin launch wrappers.

Every GPU path goes through here: every HybridModel family, Dsv4Gpu and Qwen4ExpGpu. So the file default is all families, routes and devices, and precision comes from the regions.

**Hot paths.**
- **Dense and quant matmul.** `matmul` (lib.rs:18061) is the dispatch hub for every family. The order is:
  - prefill hooks first (FP8, FP8-block, f16 mirror, MMQ, int8 MMA GEMM, FP4 opt-in; lines 18110-18169), all at m>=16 unless the verify-exact scope is set (lib.rs:18080);
  - then m=1 MMVQ decode and the batched m=2..16 tier (lines 18178-18264), which must match the decode class bit for bit (the parity law);
  - then dp4a, the Stage-A oracle and float arms (lines 18285-18402).

  `matmul_pre` (lib.rs:18462) and `matmul_decode_exact` (lib.rs:18686) restate the same ladder. They serve fused-q8 callers and the MTP/DFlash/glm5 verify walks.
- **Norms.** `rms_norm` (lib.rs:15793) is the generic norm (116 callers). Its blockDim comes from `rms_block()`, a per-model numeric knob latched process-wide.
- **Gemma.** The gemma4 dense/moe/E4B attention prologue and layer tails (lib.rs:14957-17329) and the gelu MoE launchers (lib.rs:9609-9767) are gemma-only by caller.
- **MoE.**
  - Device-routed softmax MoE (`moe_gate_up_silu8_dev_q8`, `moe_down8_fma_dev_q8`, lines 10533-10844) serves qwen3_moe/qwen35_moe/olmoe, plus gemma4_moe for the down projection.
  - The verify-rows preclamp pair (lines 9031-9215) is glm5_next.
  - Sigmoid top-k routers (lines 7908-8043) serve the sigmoid-router families.
- **Step TP/EP.** The NVFP4 EP/TP slot launchers (lines 12233-13524) are reached only from `tp.rs` TpE4m3HostBounce. That runtime refuses any card that is not an RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell (hybrid.rs:225).
- **DSV4.** The MoE doors (lines 392-592) are read only by `dsv4_grouped`/`dsv4_gpu`.

**Device-specific parts.**
- **B200 (100a build).** About fifteen doors resolve ON only there, through `b200_posture_door_from` (lib.rs:3336) and five copies of it:
  - row-ILP twins and vrows ILP;
  - hc pre-chain register Sinkhorn, the v4 schedule and the 512 block;
  - glm5 MLA half-capture;
  - T=1 device tables;
  - the native f32 and mixes GEMV.
- **GDN chunk scan.** The bf16-HMMA default (lib.rs:1127) is a numeric class: ON for 120a and 90a, OFF on b200.
- **FA decode split ladder** (lib.rs:1729-1831). This numeric class is keyed on the SM count of CUDA ordinal 0, with 128 SMs as the threshold. Desktop and laptop 5090 therefore run different programs.
- **Portable lane** (sm_89) gating is at lib.rs:1089.
- **Arch guard.** It admits (120a, 12.1) for SASS-only sm_120a fatbins (lib.rs:3755).

**Effect kinds.** Most arms are numeric, or carry numeric conservatively when a "bit-identical" claim rests on a gate rather than on construction. The workspace pools (`VerifyWs`, lib.rs:2165) are memory. Counters and traces are observability (exact items).

**Why the impact set is wide.** There are three reasons:
1. `Engine::func`, `Engine::new`, `matmul*` and `rms_norm` are shared by every family.
2. Several per-model defaults are process-global statics written by the loader and latched in OnceLocks (`RMS_BLOCK_DEFAULT`, `FA_VEC_MIN_DEFAULT`, `FA_V4_MAX_DEFAULT`, `FA_SP_GEMMA`, `FUSED_MR1_DEFAULT`, `MMQ_SK_FORCE`). In a multi-model server process (worker.rs:26127), one model's load changes another model's numeric program.
3. `arm_step37_serving_defaults` (lib.rs:1582) arms W8 mirrors, the grouped prime and the SWA ring for the whole process. Its consumers sit in the generic bf16 GEMV (lib.rs:26220) and glm5 KDA (kda.rs:1850).

Keying those on the loaded model would remove a large set of cross-family cells.

**Top findings.**
- **(high) Cross-model latch of per-model numeric defaults** (lib.rs:1725). With gemma plus qwen in one process, qwen runs rms_norm at blockDim 1024 and takes the vec FA decode below t_kv 96.
- **(medium) step37 process-wide arming** reaches other families' bf16 GEMV and KDA.
- **(medium, plausible) Missing out_f floor in `matmul_pre`** (lib.rs:18532). A small-out_f tensor gets a different prefill program than it does through `matmul`.
- **(medium) Four copies of the batched-tier admission predicate.** The parity law depends on them staying identical, and they already drift.
- **(medium) Ten default-OFF doors past their decide-by date** (Sept 19-21) with no receipt.
- **(medium) Fourteen launchers with no caller.**
- **(low)**
  - The ILP counter rises on the tmaj arm (lib.rs:9157).
  - The `set/clear_dsv4_moe_fused_for_gate` seam, which KERNELS.md documents, has no caller.
  - Many doc comments are attached to the wrong door.
  - Five copies of the 100a tri-state parser.
  - The FA split depends on the SM count of ordinal 0, not the engine's own card.
