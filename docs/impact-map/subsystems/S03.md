## hybrid_forward.rs, lines 1-16407 (slice S03)

This half of `crates/memra-engine/src/hybrid_forward.rs` holds HybridModel's prefill and forward programs and the shared MoE FFN dispatch. Five subsystems share it:

1. **Prime scheduling helpers** (1-1835): chunk geometry, PP microbatch schedule, GDN grid alignment, admission workspace shapes, and the MoE env doors.
2. **mHC (hyper-connection) walks** (2167-5175): glm5_next only, since `self.hyper` is Some only for HyperConnections plans and deepseek_v4 runs on Dsv4Gpu.
3. **Serial-trunk drivers** (5177-8704): `forward`, `forward_last`, `prime_cache*`, `prime_chunk`, `prime_layers`, the step35 batched prime and the generic concat prime.
4. **Mixers**: full-attention prime (8716-9045), GDN linear-attention prime (9054-9577), MLA/DSA (9693-11869) and stateless GDN (11872).
5. **MoE FFN** (11996-16407): `moe_ffn_inner` and `moe_ffn_sequential_zq8` plus the glm5 TP/EP walks.

### Hot paths by family and route

- **Prime, all serial families.** `prime_cache` -> `prime_cache_overlaid_inner` (5629) is the entry the server reaches for every prompt. From there:
  - hc plans divert to `prime_cache_hyper` (5642);
  - step35 takes the B=1 GEMM prime through `step35_prime_cache_batch` (5691-5747), default on because step37 defaults arm `MEMRA_STEP_GEMM_PRIME`;
  - gemma E4B and gemma vision take their own arms (5782-5796);
  - everything else goes through `prime_chunk_ranges` and either one `prime_chunk` (5873) or the PP pipelined arms (5879-5935), else the serial chunk loop (5936).
- **`prime_layers` (6763-7258)** is the per-layer body for qwen3, qwen3_moe, qwen35, qwen35_moe, olmoe, minimax_m3, hy3, glm_dsa, step35 and llama_dense. Mixer dispatch: Full -> `full_attn_prime` (step35 diverts at 8729), Linear -> GDN prime, Mla -> `mla_attn_cached`. FFN dispatch: dense f16-slab GEMMs, or `moe_ffn_il_prefill`.
- **Batched cold prime.** `prime_cache_batch_inner` (8320) serves the dense/GDN/full-attention families. It refuses gemma, sends step35 to its own walk, and leaves MLA/KDA unimplemented.
- **glm5_next.** Prime goes through `prime_cache_hyper` (chunked, ppN, B200 v2 doors). Decode goes `decode_step_hyper_output` -> `hyper_range_decode`, where the decode-graph door is default ON (2818) and the eager walk sits behind the WS / symmetric-TP / Q8-fuse doors, all default OFF. Batched decode uses `hyper_batch_range_decode`.
- **MoE.** `moe_ffn_inner` (12136) is the one entry for decode (`moe_ffn_il_zq8`), verify/MTP (`moe_ffn_il`), prime (`moe_ffn_il_prefill`) and forward.rs (`moe_ffn`). Arms in order: glm5 TP split/EP (12161), Step TP/EP (12174), step35 sigmoid-resident device arm (13136), glm5 grouped prefill (13166), the `MEMRA_MOE_GROUPED` research arm (13187), then the sequential dispatcher. Inside the dispatcher:
  - softmax families take pairs (t>16) or the dev arm (t<=16);
  - glm5_next takes device-table routing under capture, vrows and the fused epilogue;
  - Hy3 can take CPU experts;
  - everything else falls to slab-fused/gdec or the per-expert slab/SLRU loop (the spill route).

### Device-specific parts

- `pp_f16_enabled` (f16 prime fusion: ON on h100, capacity-keyed elsewhere) gates the f16out epilogues and S-glue fusion.
- `gdn_mma_enabled` changes the GDN head layout (`gdn_hk`, 6532). b200 and sm89 take the broadcast arm.
- `gdn_wgmma_on` (h100) adds bf16 q mirrors.
- `portable_mma_gated` keeps sm89 off the MLA tensor-core prefill (10126).
- `qwen_prime_graph::eligible` (6712) is desktop rtx5090 only.
- Step TP/EP runs only on RTX PRO 6000.
- `MEMRA_B200_PRIME_V2` is env-keyed, not arch-keyed.

### Effects and width

Almost everything here is numeric. Pure observability is limited to the A4 `[prime-row]` receipt, prime trace and anatomy, MoE stats/trace and the TP log-once helpers. Admission shapes (2441, 2479) are policy and memory.

The impact set is widest at `prime_cache_overlaid_inner`, `prime_layers`, `prime_chunk_tokens` and `moe_ffn_inner`/`moe_ffn_sequential_zq8`: any edit reaches every HybridModel family on prime, and every MoE family on decode, verify and prime. The two MoE functions mix family-specific arms in one item (decoupling findings at 13623 and 12174), and the glm5 walks share the file with the serial trunk (2171).

### Top findings

1. **High.** The PP-2 pipelined prime arm (5879) has no gemma exclusion. A gemma4 PP-2 prompt of 256+ tokens runs `prime_layers`, the serial non-gemma program with an unscaled embedding (7572), and silently produces wrong output. `prime_chunk` (6654) shows the exclusion was intended.
2. **Medium.** With `MEMRA_MOE_EXPERT_RP=1`, the per-expert slab read (14625/14649) and the slab-fused pair (14397) read repacked V2 slabs with the interleaved qtype.
3. **Medium.** `MEMRA_STEP35_PRIME_BATCH=0` breaks every step35 single-session prime (5711 -> 8082).
4. **Low.** PP prime loops skip the memra#536 cancellation point (2353, 5966).
5. **Low.** `forward`, `forward_last` and `prime_chunk_captured` duplicate the dense FFN and disagree on the A4 prefill program (5257 vs 5389).
6. **Low.** Several stale flags (SHEXP_DEV1, MOE_PREFETCH, PRIME_SEG, MOE_GROUPED, GLM5_TP_SYMMETRIC), a stale MLA TC prefill comment (10102), and doc blocks attached to the wrong items (2414-2441).
