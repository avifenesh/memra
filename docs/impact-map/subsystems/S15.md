## S15: CUDA attention and core kernels

**What it is.** Five CUDA translation units and one header:
- `flash_attn.cu` (10.1k lines) holds every HybridModel attention program: KV append/quantize, FA prefill, decode, verify and batch.
- `kernels.cu` (5.8k lines) holds generic glue, plus the qwen4_exp eager program.
- `mla_attn.cu` (4.1k lines) is the glm_dsa/glm5_next MLA and DSA core.
- `fa3_prefill.cu` is the Hopper TMA/wgmma FA.
- `fattn_vendor.cu` is comments only and never built.
- `memra_pdl_chain.cuh` is the dsv4/TP PDL launcher.

Liveness is uniform across the six build variants for every file except `fa3_prefill.cu`, which is a stub off 90a. Device narrowing therefore comes from Rust predicates, not from the preprocessor. The exceptions are the two PDL macros (`kernels.cu:15`, `flash_attn.cu:59`) and the KV-format macros.

**Two modules from one file.** `flash_attn.cu` is compiled twice (`build.rs:295`, `build.rs:356`):
- The default module (q8_0 K / q5_1 V) serves every attention family.
- The kf8vf8 module (e4m3) is loaded through `func_g`/`fa_func` (`lib.rs:4078`, `lib.rs:4088`) for gemma GKV/WKV layers only.

Every `#if MEMRA_KV_KFMT == 1` / `MEMRA_KV_VFMT == 2` arm is gemma plus kv_e4m3. Kernel names are mostly built at runtime (`lib.rs:28314`, `29464`, `29789`), so the stage-0 graph called 15 live kernels dead, including the default prefill kernels. All 15 were rejected as dead.

**Hot paths by family and route.**
- **Prime.** Full-attention layers of qwen3/qwen3_moe/qwen35/qwen35_moe/olmoe/minimax_m3/hy3/step35/llama prime through `fa_dequant_kv_ws_bf16` and `fa_prefill_qw_db` (hd256) or `_db_hd128` (`hybrid_forward.rs:8949`). Other head dims, `MEMRA_NOFA` and sm89 take naive SDPA.
- **Gemma prime.** Gemma uses its own family:
  - The SWA hd256 layers run `fa_prefill_w_bf16_p1h2` when `MEMRA_DRAFT` is unset (f16-P/V class) and `_p1` under spec serving (`lib.rs:1237`, `28589`).
  - The hd512 globals run `fa_prefill_bf16_hd512_sp16h2` (plain) or `sp16/sp16w4` (spec).

  This is a numeric class keyed on serving intent: gemma plain and gemma_spec cells exercise different prefill kernels.
- **step35 windowed prime.** `fa_prefill_qw_db_w_hd128` (`lib.rs:29872`).
- **glm5_next prime.** `fa_mla_gathered_bf16` (default ON, `hybrid_forward.rs:859`).
- **Decode, t=1.**
  - hd256 inside the v4 window uses `fa_decode_vec_q_v4_deep` (`lib.rs:30376`).
  - hd%128 uses `v3`, the default for every hd128 family.
  - Other dims use `v2`; hd512 gemma globals use `dpl16`.
  - Below the vec floor, decode is scalar `fa_decode_f32`.
  - Graph decode uses the `_dc` twins, and `graph_update::fa_plan` rewrites their nodes by name.
- **Verify rows** (mtp, dflash, gemma_spec): `rows_v4/v3/v2`; gemma globals use `rows_v4_512_tb`.
- **Batched decode:** `fa_decode_vec_q_seqs_v4` (hd256 only).
- **step35 TP/graph/MTP:** the `dcw` family plus `fa_decode_combine_gate_f32` (`flash_attn.cu:9297-9855`, `6528`).
- **qwen4_exp:** `kernels.cu:3227-5792` is the whole qwen4_exp program, plain and tp.
- **Gemma-only glue in kernels.cu:** layer-tail norm fusions, GeluTanh and softcap at lines 1489-2013.
- **DFlash2 drafter in kernels.cu:** lines 2388-2760.

**Device-specific parts.**
- **Desktop RTX 5090 only.** The replay-table and FA2 kernels (`flash_attn.cu:9858-10092`) are reached only on 120a builds with 170 SMs and the 27B qwen35 shape (`qwen_prime_graph.rs:100`). FA2 additionally needs `MEMRA_PRIME_ATTN_FA2=1`.
- **H100 only.** FA3 runs by default on hd256 causal fresh prefill (`lib.rs:28229`).
- **B200 only.**
  - The DSA decode door (default level 2) and the DSA select door (`mla_attn.cu:2640-4008`).
  - The hc-mixes and f32-row GEMVs (`kernels.cu:859-939`), via the B200 posture doors.
- **Not on sm89.** `portable_mma_gated` sends every f32-input FA prefill to naive SDPA. The gemma pre-staged FA prefill wrappers and `mla_attn_gathered_tc` skip that check (finding).
- **PDL entry.** It is live on 120a and 100a only. On 90a the macro is empty while the PDL launch attribute is still applied (finding).

**Effect kinds.** Nearly every kernel is numeric. Many twins are documented as bit-identical, so their effect is speed:
- `qw` vs `qw_db`;
- `g4` vs `g4o2`;
- `bf16kv` vs f32 staging;
- the `hs2` and `u8` dcw twins;
- the DSA select door.

Class-changing arms are numeric: FA3, f16-P/V, the hd512 single-pass kernels, the v4 vs v3 decode family, FA2, and the DSA warp-online kernels. `fa_decode_vec_q_v3_dcw_prof` is observability. `fattn_vendor.cu` and three dead kernels have no effect.

**Where the impact set is wide, and why.**
- The KV append/quantize and decode helper bodies are shared by every attention family and every route that touches KV.
- `kernels.cu` norms and rope serve all HybridModel families, drafters and vision towers.
- FA tuning defaults are process globals written at model load and latched in OnceLocks (`hybrid.rs:5592`, `lib.rs:2222`). In a multi-model server, one model can change another model's attention kernel. A gqa>8 model loaded after any FA dispatch can still land on the v4 kernel it overflows.

deepseek_v4 is outside this slice except through `memra_pdl_chain.cuh` (Dsv4Gpu calls no Engine kernel). The name edges from `hybrid.cu`/`qmatvec.cu` to `ld_A`/`warp_reduce_sum` are same-name per-TU copies, not real edges.

**Top findings.**
1. H100 PDL contract gap (`kernels.cu:15`, `lib.rs:5211`).
2. Per-model FA defaults leak across co-served models, and the gqa guard can be bypassed by load order (`hybrid.rs:5592`).
3. Gemma and MLA pre-staged prefill bypass the sm89 portable gate (`lib.rs:28429`).
4. Receipted-negative `MEMRA_FA_UNROLL`/`HSPLIT` doors still ship kernels.
5. `MEMRA_PRIME_ATTN_FA2`, `MEMRA_PRIME_KV_T3` and `MEMRA_KPOOL_SELECT_V2` are past their decide-by dates.
6. Dead kernels: `rows_reg_w_i2`, `rows_dpl16_kv`, `argmax_logits_f32_to_u32`.
7. Stale docs: the FA3 header says opt-in, an MLA TC comment says default OFF, the `fa_v4_at` doc is wrong, a misplaced hd512 banner, and `graph_update` matches a nonexistent `fa_decode_vec_q_dc`.
8. Decoupling: move the qwen4_exp kernels out of `kernels.cu`, and stop the dsv4 PDL global from driving `tp_ar` launches.
