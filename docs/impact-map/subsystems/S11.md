## S11: speculative routes (dflash, glm_spec, gemma_spec, glm5 decode graph, mimo MTP)

### What the slice does
This slice holds the drafter-side halves of four speculative programs and one decode-graph mechanism.

- `dflash.rs` (8.6k lines) is the DFlash/DSPARK drafter: loading (`DflashDraft::load`, dflash.rs:1548), the drafter KV with its live ring and prefix tails (2968, 4765-4978), the tap prime (5172-5376), and the served `dspark_spec_session_*` bursts (5413-6604).
- `glm_spec.rs` plus `glm_spec/prime.rs` run the glm5_next T-parallel verify, rollback and accept loop over the HyperConnections trunk. The draft comes from the embedded MTP head or the DFlash2 drafter.
- `gemma_spec.rs` is the Gemma assistant-drafter route.
- `glm5_decode_graph.rs` captures and replays the glm5 T=1 KDA runs.
- `glm5_sel_ledger.rs` is a gate instrument.
- The two `mimo_mtp_*` files are probe-only and have no serving caller (mimo_mtp_forward.rs:8).

### Hot paths per family and route
- **qwen35 / qwen35_moe / qwen4_exp, route dflash (default OFF, MEMRA_DSPARK_SPEC).** Prime goes through `dspark_prime_start` and then `DflashTapWalker`, which calls `prime_cache` with a tap sink (dflash.rs:5289). Decode goes through `dspark_spec_session_burst_inner` (6021). Its verify arms are the target numeric program, so they are numeric even for greedy requests. The proposal arms (sampled 6191, DFlash2 6210, markov 6218) and the confidence window (6254) move acceptance. On sampled requests they also move the realized stream. `generate_spec_dspark` and `generate_spec_dflash` are reached only by gate bins.
- **glm5_next, route glm5_spec (default OFF, MEMRA_GLM5_SPEC, glm_spec.rs:213).**
  - The verify walk `glm5_verify_range` (1344) runs batched by default (1365) or per row.
  - The ppN split is at 1307.
  - The draft source arms are at 3436.
  - Penalty and accept are at 3638 and 3683.
  - Two parts of this module run on default glm5 serving: the `glm5_hc_tap*` hooks (an Option check on every hc prime, 1627) and `Glm5PlainPrimeState` in prime.rs:571, the default text-only glm5 prime.
- **glm5_next, plain and graph (door default ON).** `hyper_range_decode` takes `hyper_range_decode_graphed` (glm5_decode_graph.rs:955) whenever `glm5_decode_graph_ready` admits the range. Admission requires hyper, a sigmoid router and KDA runs, which together exist only on glm5_next (834-858). This is the one default-reached numeric/speed surface in the slice.
- **gemma4_dense / gemma4_moe, route gemma_spec (default OFF, MEMRA_DRAFT).** The served path is `GemmaDraft::load`, then `gemma_spec_session_new`, then `_burst` (1798) or `_burst_sampled` (2048). `generate_spec_gemma` (729) and `gemma4_generate_plain_graph` (2240) are reached only by bins.

### Device-specific parts
Only the decode graph has a device-keyed default. The MLA-halves run plan (glm5_decode_graph.rs:276) uses `b200_posture_door_from`, so it is ON on 100a builds and OFF elsewhere. Every other arm in the slice is env- or plan-keyed with `["*"]` devices. The `cfg(memra_cutlass)` hits flagged as arch scatter are struct-field initializers, not device branches.

### Effect kinds
- numeric: verify walks, accept rules, boundary-token draws, prime with taps.
- speed: drafter arms, graph capture.
- memory: drafter KV ring and tails, q4rp mirrors.
- policy: door predicates, `dspark_vg_admission_debt`.
- observability: the selection ledger, alloc tracing.

### Where the impact set is wide, and why
1. `dspark_vg_admission_debt` (dflash.rs:5652) runs in the worker admission for every HybridModel (worker.rs:28585). It charges the MTP verify-graph pool too, so it is all-family policy.
2. `dspark_accept_sampled` (dflash.rs:1225) is the shared rejection walk. Because gemma_spec calls it (gemma_spec.rs:2172), dflash.rs reaches gemma4.
3. `graph_census` in glm5_decode_graph.rs:643 gates the qwen35 carried-prime graph (qwen_prime_graph.rs:349), so the glm5 graph file reaches qwen35 prime on the desktop 5090.
4. glm5_sel_ledger hooks sit in the generic MoE `moe_ffn_sequential_zq8`, so every MoE family pays an env read per layer while the instrument is off.

### Top findings
- **Panic (medium):** `(m + 2).clamp(3, vt_cap)` (dflash.rs:6593) panics when MEMRA_DFLASH_VERIFY_T=2, a value the parse at 6053 admits. The parse is repeated five times.
- **Decoupling:** move the sampled accept walk out of dflash.rs, `graph_census` and the capture helpers out of glm5_decode_graph.rs, and the plain glm5 prime walker out of glm_spec/prime.rs.
- **Stale doors:** MEMRA_GLM5_VERIFY_GRAPH, MEMRA_GLM5_SPEC_DEV_IO and MEMRA_GLM5_GRAPH_MLA_MID are past their 2026-09-19 decide-by date. MEMRA_DFLASH_FA keeps a kernel arm its own comment calls unfixed.
- **Documented panic and tear:** the defects behind MEMRA_GLM5_DRAFT_TAPS_DEVICE=1 remain.
- **Dead code:** 17 accessors are dead or test-only, including `DsparkHarvest::resolve`, whose doc still claims to be the served resolution. Several rustdoc blocks attach to the wrong item.
- **Spurious graph edges:** the stage-0 graph links `DflashKv::row_bytes` to cutlass_ffi through name-fallback edges. Those edges are false and should not widen the impact set.
