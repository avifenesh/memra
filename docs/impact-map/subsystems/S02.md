## S02: Engine primitives, second half of lib.rs (lines 19198-36175)

This slice holds the device primitives every HybridModel forward is built from. There is almost no model logic here. Reach is set by **weight format, head dim, KV format and build arch**, not by family name. The few family-local blocks are those whose only callers are family code.

### Quantized matvec and GEMM (19198-24614)
- **m=1 decode.** `qmatvec_mmvq_into` (22373) is the single MMVQ dispatch. Its kernel table (22525-22562) fixes the decode numeric program for every quantized trunk (NVFP4, Q8_0, Q4_0, K-quants, e4m3). The policy around it is mostly speed-only:
  - rows-per-warp choices: NVFP4/Q5_K mr2, Q4_0 mr1, Q8_0 rp mr1, and Q5_K `il` above 65536 rows (22436-22492);
  - the SM-count-keyed Q8_0 g2 grid fill (22393);
  - PDL launch arms (22582-22667).
- **B200-only levers** sit behind `MEMRA_B200_MATVEC_ARM` (off) and `MEMRA_NVFP4_ROW_ILP`, which is default ON on 100a builds (22504-22530).
- **Fused pairs and triples.** NVFP4 `matmul_pre_dual_noscale`, `matmul_nvfp4_fused2/3/4` and Q8_0/e4m3 `*_fused*_core` are format-keyed and reach all families. Two of them are family-local:
  - the Q4_0 fused launchers (20134-20806) are called only from gemma4 attention and tail code;
  - `e4m3_fused6_into` and `kda_proj_fused6_q8rp_raw_pre` are called only from kda.rs (glm5_next).
- **m=2..16 verify and batch tier.** `batched_kernel_name` + `batched_variant` + `qmatvec_mmvq_batched` (23514-23987) pick variants by SM count. They are claimed bit-identical, so the effect is speed. Kernel names are computed at runtime (23879, 23950); those edges appear only in dynamic_edges.
- **Prefill.** `gemm_supports` admits the int8 tensor-core GEMM everywhere except sm89 (24398). Hopper alone gets the wgmma Q8_0 arm (24449). `try_fp4_gemm` is opt-in and 120a-only (24030). `try_e4m3_blk_prefill` runs the FP8 MMQ tile first and falls back to a dequant to Q8_0 (22054).

### BF16 and F32 linears (24695-27549)
- **BF16 rows.** `matvec_bf16_rows_into` (26497) stacks several doors:
  - the W8 q8_0 mirrors for glm5_next and step35 (default off);
  - the tcols weight-once class for t=2..16 (default on);
  - three B200 arms (off).
- **Verify rows.** `matmul_rows_exact` (26903) is reached only by MLA, KDA and the glm5 verify head, so its families are glm5_next and glm_dsa.
- **F32 linear: the widest device-specific default in the slice.** `linear_device_into` (27509) routes every f32 `Engine::linear` through `gemv_f32_rows` by default on sm_100a (`f32_gemv_kernel_on`, 35846). So on b200 the numeric class of routers, hc mixes and vision linears changes for every family.

### Attention (27551-32739)
- **FA prefill.**
  - `fa_prefill` (28191) serves every full-attention prime and the DFlash2 drafter.
  - sm89 falls back to naive SDPA; FA3 is the default on Hopper.
  - The windowed and hd512 family (28377-29245) is gemma prime, but it is also reused by MLA gathered prefill (`mla_attn_gathered_tc`), so glm5_next and glm_dsa are in its set.
- **Quantized-KV prime.** `fa_prefill_view_ws` (29625) contains the qwen35 27B FA2 door. That door is off and reached only on a desktop RTX 5090 (120a + 170 SMs + `MEMRA_PRIME_CHUNK=1024`).
- **FA decode.** `fa_decode_kvmod_view`, `_rows`, `_rows_dc`, `_dc` and `_batch_seqs_v4` are called only from full-attention layers. Their families exclude glm5_next, glm_dsa, deepseek_v4, qwen4_exp and mimo_v2.
  - Inside, head dim picks the arm: hd512 dpl16 is gemma globals, hd256 is v4, hd%128==0 is v3.
  - `fa_split_keys` sets a numeric class by SM count: the 82-SM laptop 5090 and the >=128-SM cards run different ladders.
  - `func_g` loads the kf8vf8 fatbin for gemma e4m3 windowed caches (4088).
- **step35-only:** the ring-KV dcw decode (32187-32683) and `fa_prefill_view_ws_w_hd128`. `fa_decode_dcw_rows` also refuses below 128 SMs.

### Graphs, GDN, misc
- **Graph capture.** `capture_graph*` (32752-32970) is the graph-route machinery for MTP verify, dspark, gemma, prime and the TP token graph.
- **GDN.** The kernels (33087-35087) belong to qwen35, qwen35_moe and qwen4_exp. The prefill numeric class is device-keyed: bf16-HMMA (`gdn_mma_default_on`) on 120a and 90a, the f32 scan on b200 and sm89, and wgmma on h100 only.
- **Other family-local primitives:** `attn_head_gate` (step35), `swiglu_preclamped` (glm5_next), and `q_gate_split` / `gated_rmsnorm` (the GDN families).
- **`KvDev for Engine`** (35898) backs every KV cache.

### Where the impact set is wide
Any change to `qmatvec_mmvq_into`, `qmatvec_mmvq_batched`, `linear_device_into`, `fa_prefill`, `fa_decode_kvmod_view`, `capture_graph*` or KvDev reaches all HybridModel families on all devices. The format, head-dim and device regions are where narrowing pays.

### Top findings
1. **Medium bug.** `w8_act` mixes two key schemes (25757 vs 25897). A t=1 call with in_f = 64k+32 can reuse an undersized buffer, giving a release-build out-of-bounds write under the W8 doors.
2. **Decoupling.** The B200 f32 GEMV door sits in the generic linear (27522). Moving it to the MLA sites cuts b200 cells for every other family.
3. **Stale doors.** `MEMRA_Q80_BATCH_R2` and `MEMRA_E4M3_BATCH_R2` (decide-by 09-19) and `MEMRA_PRIME_ATTN_FA2` and `MEMRA_PRIME_KV_T3` (decide-by 09-23) are past their dates.
4. **Low bugs:**
   - `fa_decode_dcw_rows` picks the combine kernel and its smem size from different reads of the env (32452/32458);
   - `fa_decode_rows` picks v4 from the last row only (30820);
   - `MEMRA_BVAR` can force an rp kernel onto GGUF-layout bytes (23850).
5. **Dead code.** 15 launchers have no caller, plus the bench-only MoE v2 twins. Six kernels are reachable only from those dead launchers.
