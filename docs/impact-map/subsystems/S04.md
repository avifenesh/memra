## S04: hybrid_forward.rs (16408-end) and hybrid_forward/glm5_prime.rs

### What the slice is
This slice is the second half of the HybridModel forward. It holds five groups of code:

1. **MoE dispatch internals.** These sit behind `moe_ffn_inner` (first half): routers, the shared expert, and the resident, SLRU, fused and grouped expert arms.
2. **The shared activation seam `ffn_act_lim`** (hybrid_forward.rs:17273). It has 26 callers, from dense FFNs through MTP heads.
3. **The full Gemma-4 program** (22043-26339).
4. **The step35 mixer and Step TP machinery** (26341-29336, 30798-32015).
5. **The unreachable E4B path** (29338-30218).

`glm5_prime.rs` is the resumable glm5_next trunk prime.

deepseek_v4 and qwen4_exp never enter this code: they run on Dsv4Gpu (dsv4_serve.rs:57) and Qwen4ExpGpu, and the call graph has no edge from them into the slice.

### Hot paths per family and route
- **Softmax MoE (qwen3_moe, qwen35_moe, olmoe).**
  - Prefill at t>16 takes `moe_ffn_pairs` (gate at 13737-13753). Inside it, the MMA or f16g arm (17996) is the default and dp4a decode-once is the fallback.
  - Decode, batch widths up to 16 and spec verify take `moe_ffn_dev` (13773-13803). The CSR dedup arm runs only for IQ4_XS/IQ3_S (18409).
  - Spilled experts go through gdec and the SLRU `moe_cached_gemm*`.
- **Sigmoid MoE (m3, hy3, glm_dsa, glm5_next, step35).**
  - Routing: `moe_route_sigmoid_cfg`, device top-k by default (17360).
  - step35 takes `moe_ffn_sigmoid_dev` (13136, `sliding_gated_moe`), with an FP8_BLOCK reference arm and a clamped-layer arm.
  - glm5_next takes three arms: the default-on grouped prefill (13166, 19377), the PRE-clamp fused epilogue (14240, 18858) and vrows verify (19130).
- **Activation.** `ffn_act_lim` dispatches by family: swigluoai for minimax_m3 (17284), POST clamp for step35 and PRE clamp for glm5_next (17300-17306), plain SiLU for everyone else. Any edit here reaches every HybridModel family.
- **Gemma.**
  - Prime: `gemma4_attn_prime` and `gemma4_prime`.
  - Serving decode: eager `gemma4_decode_step_h` (26135), plus a PP2 door (26145).
  - Assistant-drafter verify: 25302-26131.
  - The dc/slotted/graph generate loop (24077-25295) is reached only by `HybridModel::generate*`, i.e. run-gen and gemma_gate, not by the server.
  - MoE goes through `gemma4_moe*` (23445), never the generic MoE helpers.
- **step35.** Prime and decode attention (26434, 29148). The Step TP drivers, t-row FA joins, token graph and head split are all behind default-off doors and the PRO 6000 Blackwell-only device check (hybrid.rs:225).
- **glm5_prime.rs.** The serial prime takes either PP-N or a single chunk (149). The pipelined PP-2 arm is behind `MEMRA_B200_PRIME_V2` (94).

### Device-specific parts
- B200 `_w4` matvec twins (19006). Speed only.
- The qwen prime-graph tap fast path in `dflash_tap` (25499): desktop rtx5090 with 170 SMs and the 27B shape.
- Step TP rows prechecks, which refuse below 128 SMs (27915, 28071).
- Step TP in general, which only runs on PRO 6000.

Everything else is device-agnostic in this slice. The device-shaped choices it consumes (FA split ladder, GDN MMA) live in lib.rs.

### Effect kinds
Most regions are numeric. The following are speed only, each backed by a documented bit-identity claim:
- CSR dedup
- the B200 twins
- SHEXP split, overlap and dev1
- head split
- the SHEXP_SWIGLU_ZQ8 fusion
- the dflash tap copy forms

Observability-only regions are `MEMRA_MOE_OVERLAP`, `MEMRA_STEP_TP_TIMING` and `MEMRA_SIG_ROUTER_DISPATCH_TRACE`. `PrimeEnvironment` and `PrimeRendezvous` are policy.

### Where the impact set is wide and why
- `ffn_act_lim` and the shared-expert program (16802-17033) are family-generic seams.
- `moe_ffn_grouped_add_shared_inner` serves the step35 dev arm, the glm5 grouped prefill and the Step EP/TP walks.
- `step35_tp_decode_attn_resident_v2` is named for step35 but is also the generic TP attention decode (decode.rs:3527).

### Top findings
- **Exactness.** Gemma SWA prefill switches program at `t > sliding_window` (22407), so prefix KV depends on total prompt length. Prefix-cache restore can therefore cross programs. The fix door, `MEMRA_GEMMA_FA_ONE_PROGRAM`, is still off.
- **Doors past their verdicts.**
  - `MEMRA_MOE_GROUPED` has an unsafe, negative receipt and keeps about 800 lines alive.
  - `MEMRA_STEP_TP_GRAPH` measured -20% and keeps about 950 lines alive. It also has a shadow-cache hazard.
  - `MEMRA_GLM5_TP_SPLIT_GROUPED_PRIME` is past its decide-by with the receipt still owed, yet a served launcher arms it through its HOSTDIET door.
  - `MEMRA_SHEXP_SWIGLU_ZQ8` is past its decide-by.
  - `MEMRA_GRAPH_DRAIN` only admits values documented to crash.
- **Unreachable.** The whole E4B path (29338-30218), because both gemma packs refuse E4B configs.
- **Dead code.** The t==1 and verify_t shexp arms inside `moe_ffn_pairs` (18272).
- **Merge and reuse.**
  - Three shared-expert epilogue copies.
  - gdec f32/q8 and cached-gemm twins.
  - Five expert-CSR builders.
  - Four FA ladder copies that can drift from `fa_split_keys`.
  - The verify and batch rows joins.
  - The `gemma4_moe` verify-rows copy.
- **Docs.** Six doc blocks are attached to the wrong functions.
