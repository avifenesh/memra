## S10: speculative core (spec.rs, spec/prime.rs, eagle.rs)

**What it does.** `spec.rs` (16k lines) is the MTP/NextN speculative-decode engine and the verify machinery other spec routes reuse.

- **Serving entries.** `generate_spec_session_sampled_prime_split` and `generate_spec_session_constrained_prime_split` (spec.rs:10556, 10607) both funnel into `generate_spec_inner2` (11602, about 3.2k lines).
- **Round structure.** Each burst runs prime, then draft-graph setup (`prepare_spec_draft`, 10755), then a round loop: draft, verify, greedy or rejection-sampling accept, grammar truncation, budget clamp, commit, tail.
- **Prime walker.** `spec/prime.rs` is the resumable prime walker used when `mtp_prime_walk_supported()` holds (prime.rs:247): GatedDeltaNet + FusedAttentionGate, an MTP head, no hyper connections, no PP.
- **EAGLE.** `eagle.rs` is the EAGLE3 research lane, reached only from the `run_eagle` bin.

**Families and hot paths.** The MTP route serves qwen35, qwen35_moe, step35 and hy3. The verify funnel `decode_step_t_core_stream` (5323) and `verify_layers` (7841) split three ways:

- **step35** (SlidingGatedMoe) goes to `step35_verify_batch_layers` (5978). It uses the family-armed t-column walk (6067), the eager per-row head tail (5448), the windowed dcw draft attention (3879, 4460) and the multi-head chain graphs (13076).
- **qwen35 and qwen35_moe** (GatedDeltaNet) go to `qwen35_verify_batch_layers` (6715). The t-parallel arm at t<=16 shares its body with the dspark route. Dense FFN arms are qwen35 only (7003, 7426); MoE arms are qwen35_moe only (7447). The MTP verify-graph pool is on by default only for GDN+MoE, i.e. qwen35_moe (`vgraph_family_default`, 10483).
- **hy3** and any other family take the generic decode-exact mirrored walk (7879-8235) with `full_attn_verify` (8939) and the decode-exact head (5509).

The prime inside `generate_spec_inner2` (11906-12042) has four arms: walker-prepared (GDN), stop-segmented (cold step35 and hy3 serving), monolithic batched, and tokenwise. The draft step chooses among five arms: greedy chain graph, sampled chain graph, single-head greedy graph, single-head sampled graph, and the eager fallback. Sigmoid-router or non-resident MoE heads are refused the graph arms (10446), which sends hy3 to eager drafting.

**Shared surfaces that widen impact.**

- **dflash.** The verify funnel, `VerifyCkpt`, the slab commit (6645) and `DsparkVerifyGraphs` (2765-3476) also serve the dflash route (dflash.rs:4104, 4404). Any verify edit is therefore an mtp *and* dflash cell for qwen35 and qwen35_moe.
- **Plain decode and prime.** `spec_hpost` is read by plain `decode_step_h` (decode.rs:981) and the prime epilogue.
- **MoE dispatch.** `spec_m2` and `spec_fused_t` are read by the MoE dispatch in hybrid_forward.
- **Every graph route.** `graph_launch_headroom_ok` (680) gates the decode, prime, TP and glm5 graphs too.
- **Other spec routes.** `mtp_head_forward_mla_cached` (4057) belongs to glm5_spec, and `decode_step_t` has gemma arms reached by the run_gen and gemma_gate bins (5200).
- **Process-global step37 state.** The step37 doors are tri-state with a process-global family default (lib.rs:1591). The SWA ring flag is global too and keys the draft-KV fill chunk for every model (12532, prime.rs:338).

**Devices.** The slice has no arch, SM-count or card-class predicate. Device-specific behavior arrives through callees (fa_split_keys rungs, kernel availability, FA vec eligibility in `step35_dcw_eligible`, 4425). Devices are `*` throughout.

**Effects.** Exactness is enforced by the verify for greedy decoding, so draft-side changes (dcw, chain graphs, p-min, refresh, hidden pairing) move only acceptance and speed for greedy requests. For sampled requests they also move the emitted tokens, because the draft distribution and RNG draws enter rejection sampling. Verify-side changes are numeric for every MTP family that reaches the arm. Graph and eager pairs are claimed bit-identical, but the round-level headroom guard switches between them mid-request, so they are conservatively marked numeric.

**Default doors.** Default-ON doors on the serving path: STEP35_DRAFT_DCW, MTP_CHAIN_GRAPH, SPEC_GRAPH_FILTERED, SPEC_LEAN, SPEC_M2, SPEC_FUSED_T, SPEC_GDN_PACKED, DSPARK_FA_ROWS, DSPARK_VERIFY_GRAPH (serve), SPEC_VERIFY_GRAPH (qwen35_moe), CAPTURE_GATE, SAMPLED_BOUNDARY and PEN_SESSION. On step35 the family-armed VERIFY_EAGER, VERIFY_TCOL and HEAD_ROWS are also on.

**Top findings.**

1. **Env sampling leak (medium).** Greedy serving requests pass `sampling=None`, and `resolve_spec_sampling` (spec.rs:22) then reads MEMRA_SPEC_TEMP and the other CLI sampling envs. A stray env var turns MTP-routed greedy requests into sampled ones while plain-routed requests stay greedy.
2. **MEMRA_SPEC_STREAM (medium).** It survives a negative receipt (FLAGS.md:1093) and alone keeps about 700 lines of default-unreached verify code, including `linear_attn_verify_t` and `commit_verified_prefix_stream`.
3. **Unreachable verify code.** The generic verify Linear arm (7972-8036) and `step35_verify` (8880) cannot be reached on serving.
4. **Stale docs.** The doc for `qwen35_verify_batch_layers` (6708) and the HEAD_ROWS comments (234, 5470) no longer match the code.
5. **Stale doors.** MEMRA_SPEC_ADAPT (qwen arm refuted) and MEMRA_GLM5_SPEC_DEV_IO (past its decide-by date, and read by the qwen MTP head at 3838) should be removed.
6. **Decoupling.** Step-TP KV restore syncs on every accept for all families (3589). The step37 global ring flag reaches other families' fill chunk.
7. **Dead code.** `generate_spec_session_constrained`, `spec_session_from_restored` and `SpecConstraint::mask_words` have no production callers.
